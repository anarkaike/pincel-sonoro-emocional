# Biblioteca de receitas (Style + Exclude testados em produção)

Copie, ajuste BPM/vozes/idioma e use. Padrões de configuração salvo indicação: Weirdness 30-40%, Style Influence 75-85%.

## 1. Cura / terapêutica / meditativa
**Style:** `Healing ambient world music, deeply peaceful yet quietly uplifting, 62 BPM. Lead bamboo flute, celestial harp arpeggios, sparse delicate piano accenting key moments, never dominant. Immersive wide stereo soundscape: birdsong left, distant waterfall right, soft wind crossing. Organic textures like gently crackling leaves and feather-soft hand percussion. Passages so harmonious they feel like sweet silent clouds, then gently flowing rhythmic passages, then stillness. Deep soft male vocals, warm low female vocals, children's voices weaving. Binaural-like depth, warm analog reverb, pristine separation.`
**Exclude:** `EDM, trap, aggressive drums, distorted guitar, autotune, harsh synths, heavy percussion, fast tempo, metallic sounds`
Notas: gerar versão Instrumental para loops de sessão; estender com Extend + Callback; Weirdness 30-40.

## 2. Retrato biográfico / homenagem teatral
**Style:** `Cinematic Brazilian art-song, theatrical and deeply human, evolving 68-84 BPM. Nylon-string guitar foundation, expressive piano, warm strings swelling, organic percussion from heartbeat to quiet celebration. Rich deep male lead alternating with theatrical spoken-word passages like a stage actor narrating his own life; female harmonies, subtle choir at climax. Dramaturgy in acts: intimate mysterious opening, tense strings rupture, rebirth groove, luminous grateful finale. Wide stereo, cinematic depth.`
**Exclude:** `EDM, trap, autotune, aggressive drums, heavy distortion, lo-fi, harsh synths`
Notas: estrutura em atos da vida; spoken word para os pontos de virada; metáforas que só o homenageado decodifica por inteiro.

## 3. Raiz regional que abre para o celestial (dois públicos numa música)
**Style:** `Brazilian moda de viola meets ethereal mountain folk, warm and heartfelt, 94 BPM. Ten-string viola caipira fingerpicking lead, soft accordion breaths, unique stone-chisel percussion evoking quarry workers, gentle congada drum pulse in final section. Warm interior male vocal full of soul, ethereal female harmonies, small community choir finale. Journey: intimate roots opening into starry celestial passages, returning to earth for joyful communal celebration.`
**Exclude:** `EDM, trap, autotune, electric guitar, aggressive drums, pop production, synth leads`
Notas: percussão "de objeto" ancorada no universo do público (talhadeira, martelo) cria identidade única.

## 4. Cumbia pop jovem (LATAM festivo)

Güiro and timbales groove, warm round bass, bright guitar riffs, subtle charango and bombo legüero accents keeping folk soul. Young charismatic male vocal, playful and warm, female ad-libs, group chant in final chorus like friends on a road trip. Euphoric summer energy.`
**Exclude:** `zamba, slow ballad, heavy autotune, aggressive trap 808s, EDM drops, tango`

## Princípios transversais (o que faz a diferença)
1. Voz descrita com emoção E função (`warm interior male vocal full of soul`), não só gênero.
2. Arco dinâmico sempre no Style (`from X to Y and back`).
3. Um elemento sonoro-assinatura único por projeto (talhadeira na pedra, três batidas de palco, rugido no clímax).
4. Exclude protege a intenção: liste o clichê do gênero vizinho.
5. Presente > jingle: marca sussurrada, nunca cantada (salvo pedido explícito).
